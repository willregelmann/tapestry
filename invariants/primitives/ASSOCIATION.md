# Association

A directed connection from one memory to another, with a strength between 0 and 1. The strength is how strongly recalling the first should bring the second to mind. Associations are how one memory leads to another: a question reaches a few memories directly, and associations carry recall outward from there.

## Invariants

- **Associations carry no labels.** There's only one kind. Meaning lives in the memories, and in an association's direction and strength.
- Direction matters. The strength from A to B is independent of the strength from B to A.
- **Strength governs recall, never belief.** A strong association never makes either memory more believed.
- Associations strengthen when memories prove useful together and weaken without use. Nothing is severed outright: a weak association becomes hard to follow but isn't gone.
- The total strength flowing into any one memory is bounded, so no memory can come to dominate recall just by being popular.
- Associations exist only within a single mind. Within it, they can cross [scopes](SCOPE.md), and they are followed only when both ends are visible.
